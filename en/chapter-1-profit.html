Tax-return data from 16 countries; this is a share of firms, not of profit.

Sample denominator and Pillar Two

The denominator is the number of firms in the sample’s largest size group. The study measures individual firms’ effective tax rates and associates low rates with larger firms’ use of tax incentives and institutional arrangements. GloBE top-up tax uses the group’s aggregated excess profit and effective tax rate in each jurisdiction.
